Description

Build a comprehensive onboarding API that enables enterprise customers to self-serve account setup, user provisioning, and initial configuration. The API should support bulk operations, webhook notifications for onboarding milestones, and integrate with existing SSO providers. This replaces the current manual onboarding process that requires 3-5 days of implementation team involvement per customer.

Business value

Reduces customer onboarding time from 5 days to under 4 hours. Direct impact on customer acquisition velocity — sales team reports losing 12% of enterprise deals due to slow onboarding. Expected to recover $2.1M in annual revenue from faster time-to-value.
