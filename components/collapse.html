An incident is an unplanned interruption to an IT service or a reduction in the quality of an IT service. It requires immediate attention to restore normal service operation.
To escalate an incident, open the incident record and click "Escalate". Select the escalation path and provide a reason. The system will notify the appropriate team leads and update the priority.
P1 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and resolved within 4 hours. Automated notifications are sent every 30 minutes until resolution.

SLAs define the expected service levels for each priority. Review the current SLA configuration in Settings → Service Management → SLAs.
Assignment rules automatically route incidents to the correct team based on category, location, and configuration item. Rules are evaluated in priority order until a match is found.
